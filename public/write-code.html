How to play

Tap a glowing bed (or anywhere on the farm) and Mochi walks there.

Pick a seed, then use Plant, Water, and Harvest when the crop is ready.

The little Python line at the bottom shows the command behind your action.

You can also write the whole sequence in the Python console: go_to(1), plant(1, "carrot"), water(1), harvest(1).
