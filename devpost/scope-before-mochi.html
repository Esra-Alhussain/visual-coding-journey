See how real code becomes one working project, through a colorful roadmap, visual explanations, and your own practice.

The bigger vision stays here

A full visual programming journey: grow from a starting point to increasingly complex projects, learn how code fits together, and eventually follow the development workflow through to GitHub. This short shop journey is the first piece, not the whole destination.

Who we help

Beginners, and learners who understand individual concepts but get stuck applying them together. The exact starting guidance will be defined next.

What makes it distinctive

The same shop grows as learners edit real code. Explanations connect directly to a visible change and a working project.

The learning loop

Beginner or learner connecting the basics → follow the roadmap → repeat these connected steps:

  1. 01 · UnderstandExplore a visual explanation
  2. 02 · PracticeEdit a small piece of code
  3. 03 · ObserveSee the shop change
  4. 04 · ConnectCarry the work into the next step

Proof: change the shop's appearance, add a product to an array, remove one, and see the corresponding results in the same shop.

Now · one short tiny-shop journey

Choose a shop theme, change its appearance, and add or remove products by editing small pieces of real code. A visual roadmap connects explanation, guided hands-on practice, and the live result. The main structure ends with a final independent challenge, followed by a short reflection on progress and what the learner can now do.

Exact themes, lesson sequence, editing behavior, explanation formats, challenge tasks, hints, and reflection wording will be agreed in the product requirements. Pictures, stories, and videos are possibilities; not every format is committed for this build.

Later · grow the original idea
  • A full programming curriculum and projects of increasing complexity.
  • More visual formats, including videos, and preference discovery without a long questionnaire.
  • Alternative coding approaches, baskets, checkout, and totals.
  • Planning-to-GitHub workflows and code syncing.
  • Aquarium and programmable-robot learning projects.
  • Use what learners say they want to build or practice to shape personalized next projects or learning journeys. This future feature is separate from the current short reflection.
Cut · nothing silently removed from the vision

The full curriculum, extra projects, and GitHub syncing are deferred, not discarded. Deployment is optional. A public repository and short demo video for the hackathon are separate from in-app GitHub syncing.

Suggestions still awaiting your review

A reset option and friendly code-error guidance remain recommendations, not approved requirements. The final independent challenge and short reflection are agreed parts of the journey.
