Owner authorized the no-subscription code animation alternative and coastal sound. Open `world-map-living.html`; original `world-map-restoration.html`, current home, chapters, auth and progress are unchanged.

Rendering: WebGL samples the original abandoned-map pixels. A conservative blue/teal color mask plus neighboring-water checks limit subpixel displacement and ripple brightness to water, reducing motion on land. Green-dominant pixels receive a very small horizontal sway; sparse original foliage means this effect is intentionally limited. This is a shader animation of a static illustration, not generated video, skeletal foliage animation, dynamic geometry or a fluid simulation. Buildings, damaged wheel and lighthouse are not deliberately animated. Color masks are approximate; visually review shoreline/building edges in motion.

No external assets, calls, subscription or generation credits. `world-map-water-texture.js` stores the existing PNG as a data URL so WebGL works at file:// without a server; it duplicates the raster data and increases local payload. Rebuild with `node tmp/build-living-map.cjs` if the source artwork changes. Runtime: `world-map-living.js` and `.css`.

Controls: Pause motion / Play motion, independent sound switch off by default. Reduced-motion preference starts still and can be overridden explicitly. Animation pauses for hidden/offscreen map and restoration-vision comparison; original still image remains available without WebGL or on context loss. Motion capped around 30fps at at most 1536px texture width. Original art contrast/locks and compact summaries preserved.

Audio: locally generated low-pass noise with slow gain modulation for surf, a light band-pass breeze and occasional quiet oscillator gull-like call. Synthetic ambience, not recorded nature. Starts only through the sound switch, suspends while tab is hidden or page leaves, resumes according to user's setting. No audio analytics/storage.
