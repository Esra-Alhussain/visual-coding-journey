What will you call our show?
Ribbon and bell appear after you add them through code.

Take it from here, creator.

You know the pieces. Bring them together into a performance that’s yours.

  • Name your show and write a welcome.
  • Add ribbon and bell to your props list.
  • Walk to the stage mark, then bow.
  • Choose: ribbon → twirl; bell → ring.
  • Repeat the chosen performance three times.
  • Try both props and tap Mochi for the welcome.

Start with the values and props. Then bring together the sequence, choice and loop you’ve practiced.
