# Mochi engagement checkpoints

Created: 2026-10-17. Operating instructions for the Mochi Marketing Agent and project agents encountering shareable moments. This is a workflow guide, not evidence of audience engagement or approval to post. Applies to product planning, design, building and testing; preserves existing hackathon workflows and product approvals.

## Recognize a genuine opportunity

At meaningful milestones, assess whether the moment offers an actual audience benefit: visually interesting, emotionally relatable, useful to beginners, genuinely participatory, or connected to a verified current public question. Name that specific benefit and why this moment matters now. An internal milestone alone is insufficient.

Good candidates include a visible code-to-world interaction that now works, a clear before/after change, a beginner-relevant mistake with an understandable lesson, or a genuine unresolved choice where both alternatives are feasible and audience input could affect a named next step. Share owner experiences as owner experiences, not student testimony.

Do not create checkpoints for routine refactors, tiny spacing choices, repeated previews of the same change, manufactured drama or a vote with no real influence. Do not solicit public opinions to establish technical correctness or educational effectiveness. If comprehension is uncertain, a real learner trying the task may be more useful than a preference poll.

## Automatic local action; minimal interruption

1. Read the skill's required saved context and inspect existing checkpoint/output records for duplicates. Identify the exact source artifact or observed event and what is verified versus proposed.
2. If the opportunity qualifies, automatically create the complete package below under marketing/outputs/ in the same session. Do not ask whether to draft it or only tell the owner to stop and ask the public. If recording is not available/authorized, give exact capture instructions; never imply footage was captured.
3. Save a dated `YYYY-MM-DD-checkpoint-short-topic.md`, using a version suffix to preserve a meaningful revision. Include a stable checkpoint ID; check existing IDs first. Update the same package for the same moment rather than generating near-duplicates. Link it from relevant backlog/memory records only when the current task permits those edits.
4. Surface one concise update: why now, saved package link, recommendation and what decision/input matters. Continue independent development. Hold only a dependent consequential choice that actually requires user approval or learner evidence, not the entire project. Do not automatically wait for public responses.
5. If the moment does not qualify, continue normally. No package, interruption, or DO NOT POST report is needed for every trivial change.

This behavior authorizes local drafting during normal project work, not expanded product scope, external actions, or changes to user-approved copy. If the user limits the current task's writable files, obey that limit: do not create outputs or alter records outside it; identify the restriction if an actual opportunity cannot be packaged. The present setup changes only AGENTS.md, the marketing skill and this guide; it does not fabricate an opportunity or campaign.

## Classify and choose a format

Select one primary type and explain the fit. Secondary types are optional.

| Type | Audience benefit | Typical format |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Public vote | A meaningful open preference with feasible alternatives and real influence | Story poll or carousel with a comment question |
| Beginner-pain-point question | Understand a concrete difficulty without claiming it already exists for everyone | Comment question, Story poll or short TikTok/Reel |
| Satisfying build/prototype reveal | Make a working code-to-world effect visible and understandable | TikTok/Reel |
| Before-and-after transformation | Show a genuine improvement with comparable evidence | TikTok/Reel or carousel |
| Honest builder lesson | Share an actual obstacle, correction and useful takeaway | TikTok/Reel or carousel |
| Small progress update | Show a meaningful result or next step without inflating completion | Story or short TikTok/Reel |

Recommend one concrete format: TikTok/Reel, Story poll, carousel, or comment question. Format is a creative recommendation, not a claim about current algorithm performance. Do not force video when a focused poll is clearer.

## Required complete package

Every qualifying opportunity gets all fields below with usable copy, not a blank template or promises to fill it in later. Where assets/evidence are absent, complete the script/plan honestly and specify production dependencies rather than inventing them.

1. **Context and why now:** checkpoint ID/date, current stage, exact milestone/source, target audience, audience benefit, and why this moment is timely.
2. **Type and recommended format:** choose from the tables above. Explain briefly.
3. **Claim status and sources:** distinguish confirmed fact, approved plan, unapproved idea and hypothesis. Mark actual prototype footage, concept mockups and assets still needed. Include date/source for evidence.
4. **Hook:** exact Saudi Arabic opening.
5. **Saudi Arabic narration:** complete natural, inclusive spoken script. Use plural address; avoid formal spoken Arabic. Respect the owner's established feminine self-reference where writing her voice. Keep public audience inclusive of beginners; university students are an initial learning group. Do not change product language decisions.
6. **English subtitles:** exact translations, divided into readable chunks matched to narration. Preserve valid left-to-right Python syntax.
7. **Timed shot list:** target duration, clip/time ranges, exact screen recording or visual for each, what to show and what remains to record. Timing is a target until measured. For a carousel/comment/poll, give the complete native slides/question plus a short timed narrated version with subtitles so the requested narration/shot fields remain usable; do not label slides as recorded footage.
8. **On-screen text:** exact Saudi Arabic headlines, labels, choice wording and honest work-in-progress/concept labels.
9. **Caption and CTA:** complete caption and one clear question/action. Avoid promises of learning gains or features not approved/implemented.
10. **Story-poll version, if relevant:** exact question, answer labels, panel order and optional follow-up asking why or allowing another answer. Use a feasible number of options for the intended interface; do not claim current platform limits without verification. If irrelevant, state why instead of padding the package.
11. **What responses can teach us:** name the specific uncertainty; distinguish preference, self-report, observed behavior and measured comprehension. State selection/prompting limits. No response is a finding until actually received and documented.
12. **Decision or next build step:** identify the exact artifact/behavior and owner. Explain what different answers would change, what further evidence is required, and what proceeds if there are no or conflicting responses. For a reveal with no open product choice, explicitly name the next approved verification/build step; do not invent a vote. Public input informs proposals, never automatically overrides approved plans.
13. **Recommendation:** one exact label below, its reason, readiness gaps and approval status. Include recording/editing checks and any privacy/consent review needed before external use.

## Recommendation rules

| Label | Meaning and next action |
| --- | --- |
| POST NOW | Timely, supported and usable after owner's final review/approval. Prepare complete local work first. This is not permission to publish. |
| CAPTURE NOW / POST LATER | The transient visual/process is worth preserving, but timing, verification or story is incomplete. Specify exact capture instructions and what makes it ready later. Capture only through already-authorized means; otherwise leave capture pending. |
| ASK A REAL LEARNER FIRST | A comprehension/usability assumption needs a task-based check, not a public preference vote. Include the complete held content package plus exact learner task, neutral observation questions and evidence needed. Outreach/testing requiring external participation remains pending approval. |
| DO NOT POST | A candidate has a real underlying opportunity but this version would mislead, expose private information, overstate evidence or provide insufficient audience value. Still save the complete held package with honest wording and safe visual substitutions, mark it not for publication and identify what would have to change. Do not reproduce sensitive data in the draft. |

Do not use POST NOW to bypass a failed check, missing permission, learner-authorship rules or unverified footage. No label implies that publication, recording, outreach or research actually happened.

## Current public-conversation evidence

Before saying people are currently asking for something, investigate recent, relevant public conversations using available research tools and required external-action approval. Tool availability alone is not approval. A request for this workflow does not grant blanket permission for future external actions.

Record direct source URL, platform/context, publication or comment date (if unavailable, say so), access date, the specific observation and what it does and does not support. Check the underlying conversation rather than relying only on a search snippet. Prefer minimal paraphrase; do not retain private identities or turn a public comment into a testimonial without permission. One dated question supports one observed question, not a widespread trend. Explain the chosen recency window for the claim; use multiple independent discussions before describing a recurring pattern, and acknowledge the self-selected sample.

If research tools are unavailable, research permission is pending, or sources do not support the claim, omit the current-demand claim and label the premise a hypothesis. Finish the package using known project facts; do not block independent development, invent a trend, or request permission simply to decorate a post with unnecessary research. No research is needed merely to document an observed prototype improvement.

## Decisions, safety and saved history

- Keep recommendation, drafting, approval, capture, publication and response/results statuses separate. Explicitly note when results are absent. Never invent student quotes, chats, testimonials, metrics or outcomes.
- Do not promise to implement a winning option unless authorized and feasible. For meaningful product changes, present the proposal and obtain owner agreement before updating plans or code.
- Preserve local factual build evidence where authorized; match before/after conditions and label edited/concept visuals. Do not stage fake errors or user behavior as actual testing.
- Protect private feedback and keep BubblyCue code, assets, accounts and audience data completely separate.
- All external actions, including research, messaging, publication, scheduling, uploading and paid generation, remain pending specific approval unless already explicitly authorized for that action. Do not bypass this through another tool.
- Keep final hackathon submission copy and peer feedback learner-written under 6-ship. This automation must not disguise that writing as a progress post.
- Under normal task authorization, save package links/status in CONTENT_BACKLOG, decisions in MARKETING_MEMORY, actual anonymized feedback in STUDENT_INSIGHTS and experiments/results in EXPERIMENT_LOG only when relevant. Respect narrower file-edit limits. This guide is not a fictional activity log.
