## What we will learn — and cannot learn from a vote

Learn which emotional payoff respondents describe: nurturing a place, reconnecting characters, or welcoming someone. Use their actual reasons, including negative reactions; do not assume those motivations in advance. Also note whether they understand that code causes the change.
