# First TikTok — final production checklist

Date: 2026-10-17. Related content: CONTENT-007, [revised script](2026-10-17-first-tiktok-20-seconds.md).
Audience: beginners and anyone struggling to understand and apply Python; university students are an initial learning group.
Voice: natural, inclusive Saudi Arabic; exact owner-supplied narration. English subtitles. Founder uses her supplied feminine self-reference, while addressing viewers with inclusive plural forms. No overly formal spoken Arabic or exaggerated accent.
Status: production instructions complete; recording, editing and publication not performed. Content direction/script authorization is not publication authorization. No student evidence or results asserted. Sources: owner's voice/script instruction and current Marketing Memory.

## Record first

- [ ] Record one uninterrupted clean voice take of the three paragraphs in the revised script, exactly as supplied. Use a relaxed conversational delivery; no extra spoken intro/outro.
- [ ] Measure its duration. The cut plan below targets 20 seconds; it is not verified audio timing. If a natural take exceeds 20 seconds, retain the exact script, record another comfortable take and report the duration conflict before proposing wording changes. Do not force an unnatural speed or silently cut words.
- [ ] Film vertically, ideally 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps, with a quiet room, consistent light and the camera at eye level. Record each visual with 1–2 seconds of spare material at each end.

## Clip 1 — understanding, then freezing (target 0–7s)

- [ ] Record yourself at the laptop, looking from an explanation notebook toward an empty editor, then pausing. Capture an over-the-shoulder insert of the empty editor and blinking cursor. No third-party lesson footage required.
- [ ] Screen/visual needed: empty editor only, cropped to avoid accounts, notifications or private project names. This is your staged illustration, not a real student's session.
- [ ] Narration: تفهمون شرح البايثون، بس لما تجون تكتبون لحالكم، توقفون وما تدرون من وين تبدأون؟
- [ ] Exact Saudi Arabic overlay: فاهمين الشرح… بس من وين نبدأ؟
- [ ] English subtitle chunks in spoken order: “You understand the Python explanation,” / “but when you code on your own,” / “you freeze—not knowing where to start?” Show each in turn, not all at once.

## Clip 2 — introduce the build (target 7–11s)

- [ ] Record a medium shot of yourself at the desk introducing Mochi, looking at the camera. No screen recording needed for this clip.
- [ ] Narration: أنا قاعدة أبني Mochi، لعبة لطيفة لسه تحت التطوير،
- [ ] Exact Saudi Arabic overlays: أبني Mochi وأشارككم الرحلة / لسه تحت التطوير
- [ ] English subtitle chunks: “I'm building Mochi,” / “a cozy game still in development.”
- [ ] Keep “لسه تحت التطوير” visible across the introduction and demonstration so this cannot look like a finished launch.

## Clip 3 — code changes something visible (target 11–15s)

- [ ] Prepare the existing local Mochi prototype in an already-hatched, name-editable state. Before recording, check the actual name-change interaction works; these instructions are not proof it has been checked in the browser.
- [ ] Record the editor and character/nameplate together. Change a valid existing `mochi.name` assignment from `"Mochi"` to `"Luna"` using the existing learner interaction, and capture the resulting nameplate change. This is using the prototype, not editing its source code. Avoid resetting or overwriting progress you want to keep.
- [ ] Screen/visual needed: one real, working code-to-world action with the visible input and result. Crop without hiding the causal action. No invented building sequence or simulated student footage.
- [ ] If that action is unavailable, record two paper storyboard frames: “code changes name” then “character has the new name.” Keep the exact label “تصوّر للفكرة — مو من اللعبة” on screen throughout; do not present these frames as game footage.
- [ ] Narration: تتعلمون فيها بايثون بكود يغيّر عالم صغير قدامكم.
- [ ] Exact Saudi Arabic main overlay: كودكم يغيّر عالم صغير
- [ ] Actual-prototype label: نسخة تجريبية. Storyboard fallback label: تصوّر للفكرة — مو من اللعبة
- [ ] English subtitle chunks: “Learn Python through code” / “that visibly changes a small world.”

## Clip 4 — invite a response (target 15–20s)

- [ ] Record yourself asking the final question, facing the camera. Hold the last frame briefly with all choices legible. No screen recording needed.
- [ ] Narration: وش أصعب شي عليكم في بايثون: البداية، الأخطاء، اللوبات، ولا الشروط؟
- [ ] Exact Saudi Arabic heading: وش أصعب شي عليكم؟
- [ ] Exact Saudi Arabic choices: البداية · الأخطاء · اللوبات · الشروط
- [ ] English subtitle chunks: “What makes Python hardest for you:” / “where to start, errors, loops, or conditions?”
- [ ] No extra spoken follow/like/download request; let the learning question close the video.

## Final caption — copy exactly

تفهمون الشرح، بس تتوقفون لما تكتبون لحالكم؟ 👀 أنا قاعدة أبني Mochi وأشارككم الرحلة: لعبة لطيفة لتعلّم بايثون، لسه تحت التطوير. للمبتدئين وكل اللي يتعبون مع فهم بايثون وتطبيقه: وش أصعب شي عليكم — البداية، الأخطاء، اللوبات، ولا الشروط؟ وإذا فيه شي ثاني، قولوا لي بالتعليقات 🌱
