- Confirmed: owner is building Mochi; prototype work is documented. No student quotes, chats, feedback, engagement figures or results are used.
- Approved marketing direction: broad beginner/struggling-learner public audience; university students as an initial learning group. This changes public positioning, not devpost scope or approved product decisions.
- Hypotheses: viewers recognize the opening feeling; visible world changes help learning; this question elicits useful input. None is validated here.
