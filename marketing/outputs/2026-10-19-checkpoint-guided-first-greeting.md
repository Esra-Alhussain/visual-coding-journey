## What we can learn / decision
Responses may identify which opening details people want explained. They cannot establish mastery, reduced confusion or exam preparation. Self-selected responses and the supplied example can bias answers. Owner reviews suggested refinements to tour order/copy; no public preference automatically changes approved scope. If feedback is absent/conflicting, proceed with owner browser review. A separately approved real-learner task could ask someone to change a greeting, predict the output, and explain what the quotes do; observe rather than supply the answer.
